1 Scope
This European Standard covers methods for the calculation of the design heat load for single rooms, building entities and buildings, where the design heat load is defined as the heat supply (power) needed to maintain the required internal design temperature under design external conditions.
3 Terms and Definitions
ATD, air terminal device: air out-/inlets allowing air transfer between external and internal air (external ATD) or between separate rooms (internal ATD).
balanced ventilation: fan-assisted ventilation where the sum of all supply air volume flows equals the sum of all exhausted air volume flows in quantity and over the course of time.
building entity: certain portion of a building (one or more rooms) used as one unit by one party/occupant (e.g., apartment, office unit).
design heat load: heat flow (power) required to achieve the specified internal design temperature under external design conditions.
heated space: space which, per design, is heated to the specified internal design temperature and separated from other spaces by building elements.
unbalanced ventilation: fan-assisted ventilation where the sums of all supply air volume flows and all exhausted air volume flows differ significantly.
6 Standard Method — Calculation Procedure
6.3.1 Design Heat Load
Building (build):
ΦHL,build = ∑i (ΦT,ie + ΦT,iae + ΦT,ig) + ΦV,build + ∑i (Φhu,i) - ∑i (Φgain,i)
Building entity (BE):
ΦHL,BE = ∑i (ΦT,ie + ΦT,iae + ΦT,iaBE + ΦT,ig) + ΦV,BE + ∑i (Φhu,i) - ∑i (Φgain,i)
Heated space (i):
ΦHL,i = ΦT,i + ΦV,i + Φhu,i - Φgain,i
6.3.2 Design Transmission Heat Losses
Total design transmission heat loss:
ΦT,i = (HT,ie + HT,ia + HT,iae + HT,iaBE + HT,ig) · (θint,i - θe)
Directly to the exterior:
HT,ie = ∑k [Ak · (Uk + ΔUTB) · fU,k · fie,k]
Through the ground:
HT,ig = fθ,ann · ∑k [Ak · Uequiv,k · fig,k · fGW,k]
6.3.3 Design Ventilation Heat Loss
Simplified calculation in air-tight buildings:
ΦV,i = ρ · cp · qv,min,i · (θint,i - θe)
6.3.5 Building Time Constant
τ = Ceff / H
Ceff = ceff · Ve
7 & 8 Simplified Methods
Simplified Room Heat Loss
ΦV,i = Vi · n · (ρa · cp,a) · (θint,i - θe)
where ρa · cp,a = 0.34 Wh/(m³·K).
Simplified Building Heat Load
ΦHL,build = ΦT,build + ΦV,build
ΦT,build = ∑k [Ak · (Uk + ΔUTB) · fx,k] · (θint,build - θe)
